Data Privacy Consent

Before taking this exam, please read and agree to the following:

  1. What we collect: video and audio from your camera and microphone for the entire duration of the exam, your answers, and a log of times you leave or switch away from the exam page.
  2. Why: solely to uphold academic integrity and verify that the exam was taken by you, honestly and without unauthorised help.
  3. Who can access it: only your instructor and authorised school personnel.
  4. Retention: recordings are kept only as long as needed to review the exam and are then deleted per school policy.
  5. Your rights: you may ask what data is held about you and request correction or deletion, consistent with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and school policy.
  6. Consequences: refusing camera or microphone access means you cannot take this proctored exam; please contact your instructor for an alternative arrangement.
